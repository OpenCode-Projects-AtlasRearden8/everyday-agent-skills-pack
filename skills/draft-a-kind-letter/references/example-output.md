# draft-a-kind-letter: worked examples

Three small letters and the shape that makes them kind. Copy structure,
not sentences; the words must be the writer's.

## The thank-you (after an interview)

```text
Dear Ms. Okorie,

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me on Thursday. I left the
conversation excited about the job and the library programs you
described, especially the teen workshop.

Good luck with the grant deadline. If the position moves ahead, I will
be glad to talk again.

Sincerely,
Dee
```

Why it lands: one specific thing remembered from the meeting. A generic
thank-you says "I met you"; a specific one says "I was paying attention."

## The kind complaint (to a property manager)

```text
Dear Mr. Alvarez,

The heater in unit 2 has been unreliable since January 14. I have kept
a list of the dates it cut out while we waited for service.

I would like to ask for a repair visit during daytime hours this month
if at all possible. Tenants can reach me in the evening for scheduling.

Thank you for keeping the building in good shape through the winter.

Sincerely,
Dana
```

Why it lands: facts, a request, and one genuine closing line. Notice it
never threatens; a request that can be met without losing face gets
met sooner.

## The gentle apology (to a friend)

```text
Dear Rosa,

I owe you an apology for missing your reading last Saturday. I
remembered the wrong day, and I am sorry to have missed it.

No response needed. If you ever want to catch up, my month is open and
I will bring the coffee.

Warmly, your friend
Alex
```

Why it lands: the reason is one clause long, the repair is offered, and
the reader is released from replying.
